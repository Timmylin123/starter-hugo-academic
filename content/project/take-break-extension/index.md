A lightweight Chrome extension that encourages regular breaks while using your browser. Users set customizable reminder intervals, track elapsed time, and pause or resume the timer as needed. Desktop notifications rotate through friendly suggestions to stretch, hydrate, or rest their eyes, with optional weather-aware recommendations for an outdoor walk.
